# First hero kits and future vehicle concepts

## Live first versions

The Mini Cooper is a compact front-wheel-drive DPS with a single roof gun. The GMC Sierra AT4X fills a Tank seat with 900 hull HP, conventional steering and the shared roof gun. The CX-30 is a Support SUV. The Toyota Fortuner is the Mechanic chassis. Its assisted packs heal living teammates and its recovery ability revives eligible allies. The Land Cruiser is the Logistics chassis. It uses the shared roof gun while dedicated supply transfer is pending.

BRZ DPS keeps one primary gun and gains Guard Boost: a breakable frontal shield plus a short high-speed stability/boost window. Its grounded frontal rush can add one hit per opponent; it has no tank-style stun or extra forced knockback. Finite shield HP, directional coverage, cooldown, fuel and NOS costs keep the tank dangerous. A broken shield does not make the remainder of the stability effect disappear.

The Tank projects a turret-facing, 500 HP barrier 5.4 m ahead. Its 10 m × 3.4 m panel intercepts enemy cannon and machine-gun fire and allows allied fire through. It covers a broadside when the cannon turns sideways. It lasts until its HP is depleted, costs one litre of fuel and cannot be recast within fifteen seconds of activation. Activate it with the Barrier button, D-pad Left or the remappable P key. Stopping the engine preserves the shield during deployment. The BRZ retains its small personal guard. The CX-30 Support SUV has a two-second Dive Shield with an eighteen-second cooldown. It blocks incoming damage from every direction while the spray gun can still fire. A frontal hit at 90% of its normal top speed can dislodge an enemy flag while its shield is intact.

The abilities guide describes the server tuning for these three live kits. Other dedicated abilities and every usable ultimate action remain marked PLANNED. Passive, damage and healing ultimate charge remain the existing foundation.

## Read-only concept gallery

Open Heroes → Abilities, then browse to Concepts at the end of the hero strip. Mouse/touch can select the Concepts entry; LB/RB or Q/E cycles through hero guides and the concept gallery. Up/down scrolls descriptions, and B returns to the prior screen.

Concepts are separate from the playable `HEROES` list. They have no spawn/model ID, no owned-skin inventory, no price and no loadout or main-hero action. Opening or browsing them does not send matchmaking or hero-selection requests, change the active vehicle, or reserve a team role. Their silhouettes are design illustrations rather than substitutes for missing GLBs.

## Planned kits

**Pit Crew — area repair:** A limited repair radius supports nearby living allies while consuming a finite supply. A proposed Sanctuary ultimate briefly protects teammates from damage. Range, duration, charge cost, objective restrictions and counterplay need playtests before immunity can be implemented.

**Jammer — hacker:** A warned, interruptible lock-on could briefly cut a hostile engine and display a restart timer. Range/line of sight, immunity to repeated shutdown chains, and preserving steering/brakes are required. An area Blackout ultimate remains a design idea.

**Interceptor — offense:** One weapon and strong mobility support flanking and pursuit, balanced by low armor, fuel/NOS limits and recovery periods. An SUV or utility variant could use a short-range spray cone that hits clustered opponents. Spray damage remains planned: it needs authoritative cone/range/line-of-sight checks, finite ammo/pressure/fuel and a cooldown or heat limit. A short offensive Overdrive ultimate would need a visible heat/resource cost and warning.

These planned kits do not change current 3v3 composition, damage immunity, engine controls or matchmaking eligibility. Full new vehicles follow the current match loop and multiplayer/device acceptance gates.
