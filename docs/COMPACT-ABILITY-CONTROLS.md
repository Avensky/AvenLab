# Compact ability controls

- Reset Forward has a 1-second local utility cooldown. Pointer and D-pad Up share the same deadline; reopening the HUD does not refresh it.
- Lock has a 0.25-second local cooldown shared by pointer and R3. Cooling utility presses cannot fall through to hazard lights, dyno or downshift.
- Both utility dials animate their shared deadlines with one reusable progress hook. These are local control timers, not authoritative weapon reloads.
- The ability outer arc remains slightly thicker than the inner arc (3.5 / 2.5 SVG units). A white separator sits between their radii. The Hero footer hold effect is unchanged.
- Lock and Reset have no extra button outline or background disc. They float across the lower right and lower left corners respectively. Their progress arcs retain the white separator.
- The joystick alone owns the aim module's square footprint. Every other direct child is an absolute overlay, including future tools; it cannot grow the layout. Partial visual overlap is allowed.
- The two-column, three-row ability matrix scales to exactly the joystick height. Fire remains bottom right. Secondary controller hints are hidden in the compact matrix; titles and accessible labels remain.
- Pedals are taller and wider, with larger artwork. They match the existing enlarged ignition height and baseline. Ignition retains its preceding size.
