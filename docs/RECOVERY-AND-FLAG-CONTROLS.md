# Recovery windows and flag contact

Combat defeat or fuel exhaustion disables the vehicle for a 12-second mechanic recovery window. A visible circular countdown is shown above disabled vehicles, including self, independently of ordinary health-bar visibility. Fuel exhaustion preserves remaining HP and never awards a killing blow/death. The selected Mechanic hero can stop within 8 horizontal metres and 4 vertical metres of a teammate and use U, controller LB or the recovery button. Settings exposes the keyboard binding; custom bindings are preserved through conflict-free migration.

The server authenticates the actual Mechanic hero, model, team and world. It checks positive recovery time, actor speed at most 3m/s, resources, the 20-second ability cooldown and physical placement before enabling the target. The target recovers 30% of maximum HP (preserving higher HP for a fuel-only stop), at least 10% fuel and a one-second shield. Combat revival credits the mechanic once; fuel supply credits actual gained litres rather than a resurrection. Ordinary respawns remain a separate statistic. The profile deadline protects cooldowns across a fresh reconnect body. Downed reconnects retain lifecycle/resources without cloning old match totals into a new career source.

On timeout, recovery chooses the nearest safely supported option among own-team spawn pads and a road candidate near map centre, measured from the downing location. Floor height, wheel support and body clearance are checked; an unsafe canal, tiny ledge or occupied position never counts as a safe centre. If no candidate is currently usable, the vehicle waits disabled and retries rather than spawning into water or another player.

A solo first entrant may pick up/drop flags during warmup; this does not start the official match, advance its timer, score or award XP. The real round resets warmup flags/objectives. Contested flags use the earliest segment contact; exact ties use deterministic ordering. A dropped flag is returned by physical owner-team contact, or picked up by an opponent. It no longer auto-returns on a 30-second timer. Drops after defeat, fuel loss, reset, disconnect or a fall use the last grounded carrier position so the flag is not stranded in a canal or abyss.

The minimap starts in its normal expanded state at deployment and official round start. It respects later collapse and the player's scale/visibility settings. Carriers use a gold ring and small flag glyph; ordinary team colors remain blue/orange. Enemy carrier markers and labels follow the existing team-detection rule rather than revealing an undetected enemy.

Pending acceptance: physical controller/mobile readability and scope/drag feel; recovery-window/range/cooldown balance; safe-centre choices on each authored map; long reconnect/packet-loss and team-combat sessions. Cinematic POTG video and ranked MMR remain pending.
